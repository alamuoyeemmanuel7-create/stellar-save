## The Problem

Traditional Ajo/Esusu model:
- [✗] Collector holds all cash
- [✗] High loss rate (22% of groups fail annually)
- [✗] No transparency
- [✗] Limited to local geography
- [✗] Manual, error-prone processes

**Status Quo:** Digitized versions still centralize custody in company servers. No improvement on trust.

---

## The Solution

StellarSave smart contract:
- [✓] Pools funds on-chain (Stellar blockchain)
- [✓] Automatic payouts when all members contribute
- [✓] Public audit trail (full transparency)
- [✓] Works globally via USDC
- [✓] Atomic, instant settlements

**Result:** $20B market shift from centralized platforms → trustless smart contracts
