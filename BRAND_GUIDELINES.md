# StellarSave Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

### Mission
Democratize savings for the world's 100M+ informal savers by replacing human collectors with blockchain.

### Vision
A world where anyone can join a trustless savings group from their pocket, with guaranteed payouts and zero custody risk.

### Core Values
1. **Transparency** — All transactions on-chain, auditable forever
2. **Trustless** — No middleman needed. Math replaces management.
3. **Inclusive** — Designed for unbanked, not just early crypto adopters
4. **Community** — Users own the network. Open-source ethos.

---

## Visual Identity

### Logo
**StellarSave**
- ✨ Symbol: Stylized star + upward arrow (growth + savings)
- Primary Mark: Monochrome (works on all backgrounds)
- Lockup: "StellarSave" with tagline "Trustless Savings"

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|

### Typography

- **Headlines:** Inter Bold or Poppins Bold (modern, clean)
- **Body:** Inter Regular (readable on mobile)

## Tone of Voice

### How We Speak

**Professional but Human** — We're not a bank. We're builders solving a real problem.

**Clear & Direct** — No jargon unless necessary. Crypto language alienates our real users.

**Hopeful & Empowering** — Focus on what users can DO, not technical specs.

**Local-First** — Respect for Ajo tradition. Innovation, not replacement.

### Examples

❌ **Bad:** "Utilize decentralized smart contract infrastructure for trustless asset custody"  
✅ **Good:** "Your money stays safe in a smart contract. You control it. Always."

❌ **Bad:** "Join a DeFi protocol for peer-to-peer lending"  
✅ **Good:** "Save together with your community. No collector. No fees."

## Design System

### Button States

**Primary Button (CTAs)**
- Background: var(--accent) #e0b34f
- Text: #241b06 (dark)
- Hover: darken 10%
- Disabled: 40% opacity

**Secondary Button (Less critical)**
- Border: 1px solid var(--muted)
- Text: var(--muted)
- Hover: Lighten border/text

**Danger Button (Only for destructive)**
- Background: var(--pending) #e08a4f
- Text: #fff

### Card Hierarchy
- **Primary:** Border: var(--border), Background: transparent
- **Secondary:** Background: var(--surface), Border: var(--border)
- **Elevated:** Background + drop shadow

### Spacing
- **xs:** 0.5rem
- **sm:** 1rem
- **md:** 1.5rem
- **lg:** 2rem
- **xl:** 3rem

## Brand Dos & Don'ts

### DO ✅
- Use local language variations (Ajo, Esusu, Adashe) where relevant
- Celebrate user stories and wins
- Be transparent about roadmap and challenges
- Engage respectfully with the Stellar community
- Keep crypto jargon to minimum for public comms

### DON'T ❌
- Call it "DeFi" (it's peer-to-peer savings)
- Make promises about price/returns (it's not investment)
- Use crypto bro language (HODL, moon, etc)
- Compete on hype—compete on trust
- Ignore regulatory requirements by jurisdiction

---

## Compliance Notes

- Include disclaimer: "Not a financial product. Peer-to-peer savings."
- Clarify Stellar/USDC are not StellarSave
- Disclose founder/team conflicts of interest
- Privacy policy covers data handling
- Terms of Service explain liability limits
