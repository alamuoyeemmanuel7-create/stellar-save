## Slide 2: The Problem

**Market Size: $20B+ Annual Volume**

- 100M+ people in Africa, Asia, Latin America rely on informal Ajo/Esusu savings groups
- Current model: Cash held by collector → Human trust required → High failure rate
- 58% of Nigeria's GDP is informal; 70% of market participants are women
- **Single point of failure: The collector absconding with funds is the #1 cause of loss**

**Quote**: *"I've lost money twice to collectors who ran off with our savings." — Lagos, Nigeria, 2024*

## Slide 4: The Solution - How StellarSave Works

**5-Step Process:**

1. **Create Group** — Set members, contribution amount, duration
2. **Members Join** — Friends join via group ID, form savings queue
3. **Contribute** — Each round, members send USDC to contract
4. **Smart Payout** — When all contribute, contract automatically pays recipient
5. **Repeat** — Next member receives pool next round

**Key Innovation:** Smart contract replaces human collector. All funds held on-chain. Members control via wallets.
